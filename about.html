Why we made this website

Many people often do something called self-medication, where a person uses medicines or treatments without first getting advice or a prescription from a qualified healthcare professional. Some over-the-counter medicines are intended for self-care when used according to their instructions.

However, inappropriate self-medication can cause side effects, incorrect dosing, drug interactions, or delayed diagnosis, and misuse of antibiotics can contribute to antibiotic resistance. This website helps the viewer check on any illness they might have, by checking what symptoms they have currently.

Who this website is made for

The website was made for the common people who often practice self-medication and are more likely at risk of inapproprite usage of medicines and treatments.
